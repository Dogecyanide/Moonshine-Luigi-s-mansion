**Trial By Sunshine - ALPHA**

This is an unfinished test build. Back up `susamune.ini`, every Susamune PB
journal, and `susamune_progress_v1_a.bin` / `susamune_progress_v1_b.bin`
before installing it. Record the eight-character launcher checksum before each

## English test guide

### Scope and important history

- Confirm the Homebrew Channel metadata and launcher both say
  `V1.2.0 Pre-Release 3`, `Susamune Launcher ALPHA`, and
  `Trial By Sunshine`.
- Records must report **104 active achievements** in five categories: Times
  (29), Challenges (10), Course Mastery (28), Streaks (16), and Special (21).
- The 14 easy Pre-Release 1/2 calibration achievements are retired. They must
  not be visible or included in any category/global total, even if they were
  previously unlocked. Their persistent IDs remain reserved and are not reused.
- On Wii, existing global/regional stats, achievement state, PBs, and recorded
  time must survive the update. The new per-world attempt and finish counters
  start at zero in PR3; old sessions cannot be reconstructed. Existing
  course-time history should remain. The Records progress journal is still
  session-only on Dolphin; Dolphin's settings and PB-profile persistence remain
  supported and must not regress.
- Achievement detector results in this alpha are **not release-verified merely
  because they appear in the menu**. Report both successful and failed cases,
  especially for the bespoke Challenges below.

### Timing rules

Use the requirement wording to choose the clock being tested:

- `under X`, `X or faster`, and PB-sum requirements use the exact elapsed QFT
  result. `Under` is strict: for a `52.500` target, `52.499` passes and
  `52.500` does not. Do not judge these from a rounded HUD screenshot.
- Requirements saying `X remaining on the IGT` use the native in-game timer in
  centiseconds. `Or more` includes equality.
- Regional targets must switch with the disc: Pinna EYG is `1:26.000` on
  US/PAL and `1:29.000` on JP; Sirena 6 requires `2:32.000` remaining on
  US/PAL and `2:27.000` on JP.
- **TASbot** is labelled `13.80` in the achievement text, while its exact
  Bianco 3 Secret benchmark is QFT `13.813`. Include the stored result in any
  boundary report.
- Compact QFT has a one-frame **visual-only** correction while gameplay is
  live. Core timing, frozen results, PB decisions, stored times, and
  achievement comparisons are unchanged. Recheck live alignment and a known
  PB boundary independently.

The final RTA/QFT audit changed these Diamond targets. Confirm the menu and
detector both use the corrected value:

| Achievement | Correct target |
|---|---|
| Rope Dancer | Bianco 8 under `1:00.000` |
| Murderer | Pinna 7 under `13.000` |

Bottle Diver remains Noki 3 under `35.000` because the IL uses bottle timing,
which is not the general RTA-sheet Noki 3 timing. Boat Master remains Pinna 3
under `32.000`, and Plungelo Plucker remains Gelato 2 under `1:40.000`; Demon
is intentionally brutal. The beyond-world-record Frontier targets Shell
Shocker (Noki 6 Full under `50.000`) and Ninja Warrior (Sirena 2 Secret under
`29.000`) are also unchanged.

Loading a savestate or using Stage Intro Skip must invalidate the current
attempt for achievements as well as PBs. Turn Intro Skip off before starting a
clean control attempt. Do not use Fast Forward or other assists when validating
the prestige threshold itself.

### Catalog and menu checks

1. Open all five categories and confirm their counts add to 104.
2. Confirm each list inserts only the tier separators it needs, in this order:
   Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond, Demon, Frontier. Headers must not be
   selectable and scrolling must not skip or duplicate achievements.
3. Select an achievement and confirm its exact requirement appears. Return
   through every nested level, then close the menu directly from each level.
4. Confirm Records is immediately left of Binds. Navigate from both directions
   and reopen the menu on different tabs; the visible tab strip must be the
   same every time instead of depending on navigation history.
5. On JP, confirm the unlocked/favourite star is visible. On US/PAL, confirm
   the down hint is a readable `v`, not `ÿ`. On all regions, rates must use
   `(pct)` rather than `%`, and slash-separated counts must remain readable.

should show time, attempts, finishes, completion rate, Any% PB sum with
coverage, and All-IL PB sum with coverage.

creating separate pages. Change profile and region; PB sums must be clearly

### Persistence and regional scopes

1. In PAL, record Global and PAL values. Add a known number of attempts,
   finishes, PBs, deaths, Creation minutes, and course minutes. Wait at least
   65 seconds or unlock an achievement, exit normally, and relaunch.
2. Launch JP. Global values and unlocked achievements must carry over; PAL
   scope must remain exact and JP scope must remain independent. Add known JP
   values and repeat on US.
3. Return to PAL. Verify Global equals the regional totals where appropriate,
   while each region retains its own values. Confirm an achievement unlocked
   on one region is already unlocked on the other two.
4. Confirm previously earned PR1/PR2 calibration bits do not inflate `x / 104`.
   Do not expect historical per-world attempts/finishes: those counters begin
   with PR3, while older time remains valid.

### Streak checks

The four new Demon full-route streaks are:

| Achievement | Requirement |
|---|---|

| Tower Defense | Ricco 4 Full under `44.000`, 5 consecutive |
| Loaded Dice | Sirena 4 Full under `49.000`, 5 consecutive |

### Challenge detector checks

The bespoke detectors carry the highest regression risk. Use a clean attempt,
then repeat with one deliberate violation. Do not treat an unattempted Frontier
or elite time as a failed build.

| Achievement | Positive case and required negative check |
|---|---|
| Platform Rider | Finish Pinna EYG; another Pinna IL must not count. |
| Oh My God | Finish Gelato 8; another Gelato IL must not count. |
| Hot Tub | Enter a live swimming state in Corona; death/water contact without swimming must not count. |
| The Long Way Up | Get the Shine Gate Shine without using Rocket, Turbo, or Yoshi; activating any one must invalidate it. |
| Exterminator | Kill every Cataquack in Gelato 8; leave exactly one alive before checking the final-kill unlock. |
| Fish Finder | Finish Noki 8 without Hover; activate Hover once for the negative run. |
| NoHover | Complete GBS from Gelato 1 without touching Yoshi or a fruit; mount Yoshi or pick up a fruit for the negative run. |
| Mushroom Trespassing | Enter the Pianta 5 secret without Hover or Yoshi; use either one for the negative run. |
| Mashathon | In Pinna 6 Secret, go below Y `800`, then return to Y `2700` within 15 seconds; test one run just over 15 seconds. Position metadata is recommended. |
| Indiana Jones | Open the Rocket box inside the right bell tower and collect that Rocket; another Rocket pickup must not count. |

`Exterminator`, `Mashathon`, and `Indiana Jones` depend on particularly specific
object/state identification. Attach video and the before/after achievement
screen for either a success or a false positive. Their inclusion in PR3 is a
request for focused validation, not a claim that all edge cases are proven.

### Controls, QFT, and general regression

- Unlock an achievement without a PB and confirm the short achievement chime
  plays once for the popup batch. Then unlock one on the same result as a PB:
  the PB fanfare must remain uninterrupted and the achievement chime must be
  suppressed. Audition the selected sound on JP, US, and PAL with music and
  dialogue active.
- Tap `B+DUp` for exactly one sampled frame: keep-entry restart must trigger.
- Hold `B+Z+DUp` for one frame: it must not trigger. The second sampled frame
  must trigger full/default restart, with no double-fire.
- Frame-step compact QFT beside the native timer during live play and during a
  load freeze. Live presentation should no longer lead by one frame; frozen
  behavior must remain aligned. Confirm an old PB value and one new PB boundary
  are byte-for-byte/tick-for-tick unchanged where practical.
- Regression-test settings, binds, Shined options, PB profiles, IL recording,
  Creation, native HUD editing, savestate save/load, the warp wheel, pause, and
  launcher boot on JP/US/PAL.
- Dolphin testers should confirm JP/US/PAL BPS artifacts apply to the matching
  clean revision and boot. The BPS metadata now covers the `0x20000` mod region
  and the two formerly missing hook sites. Dolphin-only persistence buffers
  were relocated to recover blob headroom; verify PB/settings persistence and
  ordinary Dolphin behavior, not just boot.

### タイム判定のルール

実績の文章によって、使用するタイマーが異なります。

- `under X`、`X or faster`、PB合計は、正確な経過タイムQFTで判定します。
  `under` は未満です。たとえば `52.500` が条件なら `52.499` は成功、
  `52.500` は失敗です。丸められたHUD表示だけで判定しないでください。
- `X remaining on the IGT` は、ゲーム本来の残り時間（1/100秒単位）で
  判定します。`or more` は同値を含みます。
- 地域差のある条件にも注意してください。Pinna EYGはUS/PALが
  `1:26.000`、JPが `1:29.000`。Sirena 6はUS/PALが残り
  `2:32.000`、JPが残り `2:27.000` です。
- **TASbot** の説明では `13.80` と表示しますが、Bianco 3 Secretの
  正確な基準値はQFT `13.813` です。境界付近の報告には保存された結果も
  書いてください。
- Compact QFTには、プレイ中の表示だけを1フレーム補正しています。
  タイマー本体、確定結果、PB判定、保存タイム、実績比較は変更していません。
  ライブ表示の同期と、既知のPB境界を別々に確認してください。

最終RTA/QFT確認により、Diamondの2条件を次の値へ修正しました。メニュー表示と
判定の両方が一致することを確認してください。

| 実績名 | 修正後の条件 |
|---|---|
| Rope Dancer | Bianco 8を `1:00.000` 未満 |
| Murderer | Pinna 7を `13.000` 未満 |

Bottle Diverは通常のNoki 3 RTAとは異なるボトル区間を使うため、Noki 3を
`35.000` 未満のままにします。Boat MasterはPinna 3を `32.000` 未満、
Plungelo PluckerはGelato 2を `1:40.000` 未満のままです。Demonは意図的に
非常に厳しい条件です。世界記録より厳しいFrontierのShell Shocker（Noki 6 Fullを
`50.000` 未満）とNinja Warrior（Sirena 2 Secretを `29.000` 未満）も変更していません。

Savestateをロードした場合、またはStage Intro Skipを使用した場合、その挑戦は
PBだけでなく実績も無効になる必要があります。正常系の確認ではIntro Skipを
OFFにしてから新しい挑戦を始めてください。実績タイム自体を検証するときは、
Fast Forwardなどの補助機能を使わないでください。

### 実績一覧とメニュー

1. 5カテゴリをすべて開き、合計が104になることを確認します。
2. 各一覧の区切りが Bronze、Silver、Gold、Diamond、Demon、Frontier の
   順で表示されることを確認します。該当実績がないTierは不要です。区切り行は
   選択できず、スクロール時に実績が飛んだり重複したりしてはいけません。
3. 実績を選択すると、正しい解除条件が表示されることを確認します。各階層から
   戻る操作と、階層内から直接メニューを閉じる操作も試してください。
4. RecordsがBindsのすぐ左にあることを確認します。左右両方向から移動し、
   別タブでメニューを開き直しても、上部タブの表示範囲が毎回同じになることを
   確認してください。
5. JPでは解除済み・お気に入りの星印を確認します。US/PALでは下方向の案内が
   `ÿ` ではなく `v` と表示されることを確認します。全地域で達成率は `%` の

### 操作、QFT、全体回帰テスト

- PBを更新せずに実績を解除し、短い実績チャイムが一連のポップアップにつき1回だけ
  鳴ることを確認してください。次にPB更新と同時に実績を解除し、PBファンファーレが
  中断されず、実績チャイムが抑制されることを確認してください。JP/US/PALでBGMや
  会話音声が鳴っている状態でも音量と聞こえ方を確認してください。
- `B+DUp` を入力取得1フレームだけ押し、同じ入口からのリスタートが発動すること。
- `B+Z+DUp` は1フレームでは発動せず、2フレーム目で通常のフルリスタートが
  1回だけ発動すること。
- Compact QFTとゲーム本来のタイマーを並べ、通常プレイ中とロード停止中を
  フレーム送りで比較してください。通常時の1フレーム先行が解消し、停止中の同期は
