# Susamune V1.1.0 release verification

**Happy Shine Happy Life**

V1.1.0 promotes RC3 without gameplay changes. The release build changes only
the Homebrew Channel name, launcher title/version, and packaged documentation.

## Hardware validation

- The V1.1 release candidates were exercised on JP and PAL Wii hardware with
  Nintendont cheats disabled and without external Gecko codes.
- Bowser retains Sunshine's native Rocket/full-water setup, records its PB
  separately from Corona Mountain, and does not leak Rocket into later stages.
- Gelato 4 Inside retains its tested Spray/Rocket setup without leaking it.
- Pinna 1 and Airstrip 1 record their selected PBs through their cutscene
  routes.
- Piantissimo `Slowest` and `Fastest` work in Gelato 5, Noki 5, and Pianta 2.
- The established PB profile, theoretical-best, Wallkick Display, savestate
  feedback, pause clean-view, menu, factory-reset, and restart tests passed
  across the release-candidate cycle.

## Known limitation

Airstrip reset commands remain unreliable and are not a V1.1.0 release
blocker. The Airstrip IL and PB recording work correctly.
