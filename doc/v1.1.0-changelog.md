# Susamune V1.1.0

**Happy Shine Happy Life**

V1.1.0 is a refinement release focused on individual-level practice, clearer
feedback, menu usability, reliability, packaging, and careful memory use.

## IL PB improvements

- Added four PB-only profiles: `Any%`, `120 Shines`, and two nameable custom
  profiles. Existing V1.0 PBs migrate into `Any%`.
- Added independent PB recording, popup, and fanfare controls. Disabling
  recording suppresses all PB notifications, and Stage Intro Skip
  automatically disables recording.
- The Any% profile displays a theoretical best once all 55 agreed route ILs
  have PBs.
- Recent 5 can use long or compact IL names and has a complete editor for its
  position, scale, colours, opacity, brightness, background, and padding.
- Moved Recent 5 and PB controls into a dedicated `PB OPTIONS` section in the
  IL tab.

## IL additions and corrections

- Added `Pinna Park EYG` (`PEYG`) separately from Pinna 6 Full.
- Added `Noki 4 (Eel Only)` (`NB4i`) and `Gelato 4 (Inside)` (`GB4i`).
- Added `Bowser` separately from full `Corona Mountain`. Bowser retains
  Sunshine's native full-water Rocket setup without carrying Rocket into
  later stages.
- Noki 3 now begins inside the bottle.
- Corrected Pinna and Sirena IL starting locations.
- Pinna 1 and Airstrip 1 remain eligible through their cutscene routes and
  record the selected PB correctly.
- Bianco 2 can complete through the Bianco 1 route used in Any%.
- Added `Off`, `Slowest`, and `Fastest` Piantissimo patterns for Gelato 5,
  Noki 5, and Pianta 2.

## Practice tools and feedback

- Added Wallkick Display with normal, A+B instant-dive, and spin-wallkick
  detection. It reports `1st` through `6th` or `Late` and includes a complete
  editor with a separate colour for every result.
- Savestate saving, loading, and refusal provide clear feedback. The feedback
  can be hidden or fully edited.
- Added an optional queued-restart popup.
- Instant Restart works during Mario's death fade and waits safely for save
  dialogs and memory-card activity.
- Full Restart allows a one-frame grace period so staggered B+Z+D-Up inputs
  are not misread as a current-area restart. Instant commands also close an
  already-open warp wheel before departing.
- Added optional native save prompts for repeat Yoshi mounts and nozzle
  pickups.
- Added a two-step Factory Reset that restores settings, binds, and display
  layouts immediately while preserving every IL PB profile.
- Added Post-Corona Plaza to the warp wheel and preserve its state through
  restart and Area Lock.
- Z toggles a clean view while Sunshine's pause menu is open, hiding the
  native HUD and pause menu until restored or unpaused.

## QFT and timing

- Added optional QFT freezing when collecting a 1-Up.
- Added QFT section history for consecutive same-area practice sections;
  savestate restoration preserves it.
- Area loads begin a fresh section-history list while the full-level QFT clock
  remains continuous.
- Fixed final-time capture with `No shine get animation` and clean QFT restart
  on the following attempt.
- The core QFT clock, freeze hooks, and savestate state-copy paths retain their
  established implementation.

## Menu and launcher polish

- Reorganised the mod menu into clearer practice-focused pages with concise
  labelled sections.
- C-stick left/right jumps sections and A selects or changes an item.
- X stars persisted settings and IL PB options into a `Starred` tab without
  duplicating their state.
- Improved tab spacing and use of available menu space.
- Launcher packages include this changelog and release verification log.
- Added `Dogecyanide` to the on-console launcher credits.

## Reliability and optimisation

- Restored normal Mario death handling after IL completion logic intercepted
  lethal damage.
- Fixed stage-specific PB matching, special loadout leakage, Piantissimo
  pattern switching on console, Full Restart input handling, and save-dialog
  restart conflicts.
- Consolidated duplicated menu rendering, keyboard input, overlay
  persistence, IL formatting, restart setup, and mailbox staging.
- Recovered roughly 6 KiB from the largest V1.1 development build while
  retaining the release feature set.
- Builds and launcher payloads are produced for JP, US, and PAL.

## Known limitations and out of scope

- Airstrip reset commands remain unreliable. The Airstrip IL and PB recording
  work correctly; only its reset behaviour is considered bugged.
- Freecam and audio-state restoration remain future work.
