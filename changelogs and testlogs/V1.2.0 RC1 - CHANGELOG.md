# Susamune V1.2.0 RC1

**Trial By Sunshine — Susamune Launcher ALPHA**

Release Candidate 1 expands the active catalog from 103 to **123
achievements**. It adds 10 time achievements and 10 streak achievements to the
Pre-Release 4 foundation without changing QFT timing code.

## New time achievements

- Bronze — **Sand in the Hourglass:** Gelato 1 Reds `< 45.000` QFT.
- Silver — **Red Tide:** Ricco 4 Reds `< 50.000` QFT.
- Silver — **I Just Landed:** Airstrip 1 `< 44.000` QFT.
- Silver — **Hillside Hustle:** Bianco 3 Full `< 42.000` QFT.
- Gold — **Then It's War!:** Gelato 1 Full `< 40.000` QFT.
- Diamond — **Casino Royale:** Sirena 4 Reds `< 35.000` QFT.
- Demon — **They See Me Rollin' (Out):** Ricco 4 Secret `< 30.000` QFT.
- Demon — **Ghostly Reds:** Sirena 2 Reds with at least `1:11.01` remaining
  on native IGT. The unlock appears on Shine grab.
- Frontier — **Chuckster Change:** Pianta 5 Reds `< 40.000` QFT.
- Frontier — **Where There Is a Will There Is a Way:** Box Game 2 with at
  least `23.00` remaining on native IGT. The unlock appears on Shine grab.

## New streak achievements

- Bronze — **Frequent Flyer:** finish Airstrip 1 five times consecutively.
- Bronze — **Run Killer Conquered:** finish Ricco 4 Secret five times
  consecutively.
- Silver — **Sandcastle Siege:** Gelato 1 Full `< 45.000` five times.
- Gold — **Harbor Habit:** Ricco 4 Full `< 50.000` five times.
- Diamond — **Casino Circuit:** Sirena 4 Reds `< 38.000` five times.
- Diamond — **Dog Trainer:** Pianta 1 `<= 43.000` on JP or `<= 44.000` on
  US/PAL, five times.
- Demon — **Clockwork Crisis:** Pinna 6 Secret `< 27.500` five times.
- Demon — **I Didn't Hear No Bell:** Bianco 4 `< 43.000` five times.
- Demon — **Mole Mangler:** Noki 1 `< 54.000` five times.
- Frontier — **Lord of the Nut:** Gelato 8 `< 27.000` ten times. Direct
  Gelato 8 entry and GBS from Gelato 1 feed the same streak.

## Compatibility and packaging

- Existing achievement IDs and saved unlocks remain stable. The retired
  Indiana Jones slot stays invisible and is not included in the total.
- Reset Records still clears Records statistics, achievements, and live
  streaks while preserving PBs and configuration. Factory Reset still clears
  configuration while preserving PBs and Records.
- Existing Pre-Release 4 fixes remain unchanged. Its full regression suite
  does not need to be repeated for RC1.
- The release workflow now checks out the immutable release tag and packages
  `susamune_launcher_RC1.zip`.
