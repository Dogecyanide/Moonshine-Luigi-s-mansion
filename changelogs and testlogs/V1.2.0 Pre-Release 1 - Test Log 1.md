# V1.2.0 Pre-Release 1 - Test Log 1

**Trial By Sunshine - ALPHA**
**Tester:** Dogecyanide
**Regions exercised:** PAL, JP, US

## Confirmed working

- Bronze `First Steps` unlocked correctly.
- Gold `Bianco Breakthrough` unlocked correctly.
- Diamond `Village Complete` was already unlocked from existing PBs, confirming
  migration/backfill reached the achievement catalog.
- Statistics update during play.
- Creation time updates.
- Course-time buckets appear correct.
- Time played, PBs earned, and ILs finished were observed after switching
  regions, confirming that the shared progress journal is being read.
- Stage Intro Skip retains PB invalidation and also invalidates timed/challenge
  achievements.
- US progress tracking initializes even on a region not booted since the RC
  builds.

## Not practically exercised

- Demon `Fivefold Secret` was not completed because its real requirement is
  intentionally severe.
- Frontier `Beyond the Frontier` was not completed because its threshold is at
  world-record level and is unsuitable as an alpha QA requirement.

The next alpha needs deliberately trivial test achievements for validating
categories, persistence, and all tier presentations without requiring elite
runs.

## Issues and observations

- JP's system font appears not to contain the slash used by `3 / 6`; the row
  renders approximately as `3     6`.
- The PAL achievement-completion marker is also absent with the JP font.
- The menu cannot currently be closed while inside a nested Records page.
- The QFT display is visually one game frame ahead of the retail timer while a
  level is actively running. The underlying timing is accurate, and both
  displays agree during load freezes.
- `B+D-Up` requiring two held frames makes instant restart easy to miss. Restore
  it to one frame while retaining two frames for `B+Z+D-Up`.
- Savestate use should invalidate challenge/timed achievements, matching Stage
  Intro Skip's policy.
- Total playtime could count every frame from boot, including menus and loads.
  This is primarily a semantics/simplicity decision, not expected to recover
  meaningful heap or blob space.

## Persistence observations

Cross-region values were present but not fully current, consistent with the
five-minute ordinary-stat checkpoint losing the tail of a session:

- PAL showed 20 attempts before switching regions.
- JP later showed 17 attempts; deaths did not appear to carry fully.
- JP was left at 17 minutes, 26 attempts, 6 IL finishes, 5 PBs earned, and
  3 minutes in Creation.
- US then showed 15 minutes, 18 attempts, 4 IL finishes, 3 deaths, and 2 minutes
  in Creation.

The two on-SD journal generations have been preserved for exact decoding. The
next alpha should improve checkpoint/exit behavior without writing on every
attempt or frame.

## Next-alpha focus

- Progress persistence and cross-region freshness.
- Achievement categories.
- A few trivial detector achievements in every category.
- JP-safe progress and completion text.
- Nested-menu close behavior.
- Restart hold split (`B+D-Up` one frame, `B+Z+D-Up` two frames).
- Presentation-only QFT alignment.
- Savestate achievement invalidation.

## Separate release issue

The V1.1 GitHub launcher artifact succeeded, but all JP/US/PAL Dolphin BPS
artifacts failed because the stored ISO-layout metadata predates the Yoshi-ride
and nozzle-pickup hook addresses. This does not affect Wii testing. Regenerating
the three layouts requires verified clean regional ISOs before a future release.
