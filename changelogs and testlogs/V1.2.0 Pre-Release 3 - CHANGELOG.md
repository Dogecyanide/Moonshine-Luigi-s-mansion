# Susamune V1.2.0 Pre-Release 3

**Trial By Sunshine - ALPHA**

This build replaces the small calibration catalog with the first launch-scale
achievement and world-statistics pass. Achievement detectors in this alpha are
test candidates, not release-verified behavior; please use the included
`TESTING.md` when reporting results.

## Achievements

- The catalog now has **104 active achievements** across five categories:
  Times (29), Challenges (10), Course Mastery (28), Streaks (16), and Special
  (21).
- The 14 Pre-Release 1/2 calibration achievements are retired. Their saved IDs
  remain reserved and are never reused, but they no longer appear or count
  toward completion.
- Achievement lists are divided by reusable Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond,
  Demon, and Frontier tier separators.
- Selecting an achievement shows its requirement.
- Four hardcore full-route Demon streaks were added:

  - **Dirty Work** — Bianco 6 Full under `1:10.000`, five times in a row.
  - **Tower Defense** — Ricco 4 Full under `44.000`, five times in a row.
  - **Loaded Dice** — Sirena 4 Full under `49.000`, five times in a row.
  - **Shell Game** — Noki 6 Full under `52.500`, five times in a row.

- The final RTA/QFT audit corrected two Diamond targets: **Rope Dancer**
  (Bianco 8 under `1:00.000`) and **Murderer** (Pinna 7 under `13.000`).
  Bottle Diver retains its IL-specific bottle timing, and the deliberately
  brutal Demon and beyond-world-record Frontier targets remain unchanged.

- All-IL Course Mastery targets now use the paired RTA-sheet QFT values for
  red-coin ILs instead of mixing leaderboard IGT and elapsed QFT:

  | Course | Target |
  |---|---:|
  | Bianco | under `12:45` |
  | Ricco | under `9:45` |
  | Gelato | under `10:45` |
  | Pinna | under `11:00` (`11:03` on JP) |
  | Sirena | under `17:10` (`17:15` on JP) |
  | Noki | under `10:40` |
  | Pianta | under `10:15` |

Elapsed-time achievements use the exact QFT result. Requirements expressed as
time remaining use the native in-game timer (IGT) in centiseconds.

## Records and statistics

- Records now has nested pages for Bianco, Ricco, Gelato, Pinna, Sirena, Noki,
  Pianta, and Delfino. Airstrip and Corona are folded into Delfino.
- Each world page shows time, attempts, finishes, completion rate, Any% PB sum
  with coverage, and All-IL PB sum with coverage.
- Existing global and regional progress remains shared through the progress
  journal. Existing time history is retained; the new per-world attempt and
  finish counters begin at zero in Pre-Release 3 and cannot reconstruct older
  runs.
- Records journal persistence remains console-only in this alpha. Dolphin's
  existing settings and PB-profile persistence remains supported.
- Completion rates use font-safe `(pct)` text because `%` maps to a controller
  glyph or is absent in the regional fonts.

## Menu and presentation

- Records has moved next to Binds, and the scrolling tab strip now chooses its
  visible range deterministically instead of depending on navigation history.
- JP uses the correct completion-star glyph. The US/PAL down hint uses a safe
  `v` instead of the broken `0xFF` glyph.
- Tier headings, completion markers, nested pages, and requirement details are
  included in the new achievement layout.
- Achievement popup batches play one short system chime. A simultaneous PB
  fanfare takes priority, so the achievement sound is suppressed instead of
  interrupting or competing with it.

## Eligibility, timing, and controls

- Loading a savestate or using Stage Intro Skip invalidates the current
  attempt for achievements, matching PB eligibility.
- `B+DUp` keep-entry restart triggers from one sampled frame. `B+Z+DUp`
  full/default restart still requires two sampled frames.
- Compact QFT's live display is corrected by one visual frame. The underlying
  timer, frozen result, PB decisions, stored QFT values, and achievement
  comparisons are unchanged.

## Build and artifact fixes

- Dolphin-only persistence storage was relocated out of the linked mod blob to
  reclaim development-build headroom without changing QFT timing code.
- JP, US, and PAL source-free Dolphin BPS artifacts build again. Their layouts
  now cover the `0x20000` mod region and include the two V1.1 hook sites that
  were missing from the older metadata.
- The launcher package now includes this changelog and the bilingual
  Pre-Release 3 testing guide instead of the stale Pre-Release 2 documents.
