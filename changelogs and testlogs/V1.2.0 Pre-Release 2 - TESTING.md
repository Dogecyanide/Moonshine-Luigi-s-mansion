# Susamune V1.2.0 Pre-Release 2 testing

**Trial By Sunshine - ALPHA**

This is an unfinished test build. Back up `susamune.ini`, all Susamune PB
journals, and `susamune_progress_v1_a.bin` / `susamune_progress_v1_b.bin`
before installing it. Dolphin Records remain session-only in this alpha.

The eight calibration achievements are persistent IDs 6-13. They may be
retired later, but their IDs must never be reordered, deleted, or repurposed.

## Preflight and menu

- Confirm the Homebrew Channel, launcher, and Records page identify the build
  as `V1.2.0 Pre-Release 2`, `Trial By Sunshine`, and `ALPHA`.
- Confirm Records reports 14 total achievements and opens four categories:
  Times (4), Challenges (3), Practice Craft (4), and Course Mastery (3).
- Open a category, achievement list, and achievement detail. At every nested
  level, the configured Close Menu bind must close the menu immediately.
- Confirm the former `Starred` settings tab is named `Shined`. Existing
  favourites must remain selected; this is a label change only.

## Exact calibration achievement sequence

Use an eligible IL attempt with Stage Intro Skip off and no savestate load.
Start with the calibration achievements locked when possible.

1. Finish Bianco Hills 1 in 9:59.99 or less. This unlocks ID 6,
   **Warm-Up Pace** (Bronze, Times), plus ID 0 **First Steps** on fresh data.
2. Finish Bianco Hills 1 again without finishing another IL between runs. This
   unlocks ID 10, **Run It Back** (Gold, Practice Craft).
3. Finish Bianco Hills 1 a third consecutive time. This unlocks ID 11,
   **Again, Again!** (Diamond, Practice Craft).
4. Finish Ricco Harbor 1 in 9:59.99 or less. This unlocks ID 7,
   **Frontier Calibration** (Frontier, Times) and tests the green outline.
5. Finish either Airstrip IL without loading a savestate. This unlocks ID 8,
   **Cleared for Takeoff** (Silver, Challenges).
6. Finish any Delfino Plaza IL without loading a savestate. This unlocks ID 9,
   **Plaza Challenger** (Demon, Challenges).
7. Finish any recognized Gelato Beach IL. This unlocks ID 12,
   **Gelato Initiate** (Bronze, Course Mastery).
8. Finish any recognized Pinna Park IL. This unlocks ID 13,
   **Pinna Initiate** (Silver, Course Mastery).

After every step, check the outlined notification, category count, completion
marker, detail text, and unlocked/total count. Reopen Records after a reboot
and confirm every unlocked ID remains unlocked. The original Demon and
Frontier targets do not need to be reproduced for this calibration pass.

## Persistence and regional scopes

- In PAL, record every Global and PAL value. Add attempts, one death, one IL
  finish, one PB, Creation time, and course time. Spend at least 65 seconds in
  the session without unlocking an achievement, exit normally, and relaunch.
  Confirm all values survived the one-minute checkpoint.
- Make another stat change, then move through normal game flow to a different
  course bucket (for example, Bianco to Delfino) before one minute has elapsed.
  After the storage status settles, relaunch and confirm the course-change
  checkpoint saved it.
- Launch JP. Global must equal the PAL totals, PAL scope must retain its exact
  values, and JP scope must begin at its own values. Add known JP values and
  repeat the checkpoint test. Launch US and verify Global is PAL + JP + US,
  while each regional scope remains exact.
- Unlock one calibration achievement in one region and confirm its bit is
  already unlocked in the other two regions. Achievements are global; stats
  are regional and summed only in Global.
- Leave the game on logos, title, loading screens, or menus for at least two
  minutes. Total Playtime must rise because it is boot-wide, while course time
  and Creation time must not rise outside their own contexts.

## Savestate invalidation

- With **Cleared for Takeoff** or **Plaza Challenger** still locked, save during
  its IL, load that savestate, then finish. The loaded attempt must not unlock
  the achievement or earn a PB under the existing IL eligibility rule.
- Restart the IL normally and finish without loading. The achievement must now
  unlock.
- With **Run It Back** locked, finish an IL once, load a savestate during the
  next attempt, and finish. The loaded result must not complete the two-run
  streak. Two later clean consecutive finishes must unlock it.
- Regression-test ordinary savestate save/load, feedback, and same-scenario
  safety after these eligibility checks.

## QFT and restart timing

- Show compact QFT beside the native timer. During live gameplay, frame-step
  the first ten frames and confirm compact QFT no longer appears one frame
  ahead. Loading, coin, talk, pause, and other frozen displays must remain
  aligned exactly as before.
- Finish an IL near a known boundary and confirm its recorded time, PB decision,
  and achievement threshold are unchanged. This correction is display-only;
  the QFT timing core and stored result were not changed.
- Tap B+DUp for exactly one sampled frame. Keep-entry restart must trigger.
- Hold B+Z+DUp for one frame: no restart. Hold it through the second sampled
  frame: full/default restart must trigger. Check that neither bind double-fires.

## JP UI checks

- On JP, confirm slashes are visible in the root achievement count, all four
  category counts, and detail progress (for example `3 / 14`, not a gap).
- Confirm completed-achievement and favourite star markers render on JP, and
  compare the same rows on US and PAL for layout regressions.
- Confirm `Shined`, category names, requirements, tier labels, and footers fit
  without missing glyphs or corrupted neighbouring text.

## General regression and reporting

Regression-test IL PB profiles, settings/binds/layout persistence, deaths,
Creation and course timing, the warp wheel, pause/menu behavior, and all three
restart paths. Airstrip reset commands remain a known V1.1 limitation; its IL
and PB recording should still work.

Report the game region, Wii or Dolphin version, launch/storage device, exact
steps, expected and actual result, visible build checksum, and the before/after
Records values. Attach `susamune.ini` only after removing anything private.
